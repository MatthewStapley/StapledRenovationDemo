## Notes

- **Transformation scene:** the floor plan is drawn from scratch for this demo, and the elevation is traced by hand from the Poojan Thanekar image. Neither is a real survey. The scene is labelled "Illustrative concept" on the stage and in its caption. No before-and-after is shown, because no matching "before" photo of that room exists.
- **Materials:** each style is a different room. The page says so, and doesn't pretend to recolour one room. Palette swatches are sampled from each photograph.
- **Rendered images:** some images (e.g. the transformation photo) look like 3D visualisations rather than photographs. The page calls them "images" or "inspiration" and never claims they show completed work.
- **Visible products:** some images show small branded toiletries, such as the soap bottle on the hero vanity. Their labels are incidental and barely legible.
